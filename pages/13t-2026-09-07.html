1. 實用字彙與片語

  • expedite (v.) 加速；促進
    expedite the approval process｜加速核准流程
  • prerequisite (n.) 先決條件
    meet a prerequisite｜符合先決條件
  • contingent on (phr.) 取決於；以……為條件
    approval is contingent on funding｜核准取決於資金
  • allocate (v.) 分配；撥給
    allocate additional funds｜撥出額外資金
  • otherwise (adv.) 否則；在其他方面
    respond by Friday; otherwise, the offer will expire｜週五前回覆，否則提案將失效

2. 文法重點：省略 if 的倒裝條件句

正式商務英文常省略 if，並將 should、were 或 had 移到主詞前：Should + S + V；Were + S + to V；Had + S + p.p.。

Should the committee approve the plan, we will begin immediately.
Had we received the figures earlier, we would have completed the review.

3. 短文

The launch of Norvale Corporation's employee wellness program is contingent on final budget approval. To expedite the review, department heads must submit revised cost estimates by Thursday. Should the Finance Committee approve the proposal, additional funds will be allocated to fitness classes and preventive health screenings. Had the original estimates included facility rental fees, the review would already have been completed. Managers should respond promptly to requests for clarification; otherwise, the planned October 1 launch may be delayed.

4. 題目

  1. 1. What must happen before the wellness program can be launched?
    1. Employees must enroll in classes.
    2. The budget must receive final approval.
    3. A new facility must be constructed.
    4. Health screenings must be completed.
  2. 2. Why has the budget review not yet been completed?
    1. Rental costs were omitted from the original estimates.
    2. Department heads missed Thursday’s meeting.
    3. The committee requested a later launch date.
    4. The company reduced the number of fitness classes.
  3. 3. _____ the supplier offer a larger discount, we would reconsider the contract.
    1. Had
    2. Should
    3. Were
    4. Unless
  4. 4. Final authorization is _____ the successful completion of the safety inspection.
    1. contingent on
    2. otherwise
    3. expedite
    4. prerequisite
顯示答案與解析
  1. B — 首句的 contingent on final budget approval 表示方案能否推出取決於預算最終獲准。
  2. A — Had the original estimates included facility rental fees 暗示原估價漏列場地租金，因此審查尚未完成。
  3. B — Should + 主詞 + 原形動詞等於 If + 主詞 + should + 原形動詞，表示較不確定的未來條件。
  4. A — 固定搭配 be contingent on 表示「取決於；以……為條件」。
今日答題技巧：看到句首空格後緊接「主詞＋動詞」，先檢查是否為省略 if 的條件倒裝。Should 表未來可能條件；Had + 主詞 + p.p. 表與過去事實相反。閱讀推論題要把 Had...would have... 還原成 if 子句。
