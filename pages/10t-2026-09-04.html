2026年9月4日｜Invoice & Payment Communication

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • outstanding (adj.) 尚未支付的；未完成的
    an outstanding invoice｜一張尚未付款的發票
  • overdue (adj.) 逾期的
    an overdue payment｜逾期款項
  • remit (v.) 匯付、支付
    remit payment electronically｜以電子方式匯款
  • waive (v.) 免除；放棄
    waive the late fee｜免除滯納金
  • incur (v.) 招致、產生（費用／損失）
    incur additional charges｜產生額外費用

2. 文法重點：未來時間子句

when / once / as soon as / before / after 引導未來時間子句時，時間子句使用現在簡單式，不使用 will；主要子句可使用 will。

Once payment is received, we will send a receipt.
As soon as the manager approves the request, we will issue a refund.

3. 短文

Our records indicate that invoice 4837, issued on August 5, remains outstanding. Please remit the full amount by September 10 to avoid incurring additional charges. Once payment is received, an electronic receipt will be sent to the billing address on file. If you have already made the payment, please disregard this notice. Customers experiencing technical difficulties should contact the Billing Department as soon as possible. The late fee may be waived when a system error has delayed an otherwise timely payment.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why should the customer pay by September 10?
    1. To qualify for a discount
    2. To avoid additional charges
    3. To update the billing address
    4. To receive a paper invoice
  2. 2. What will happen after the payment is received?
    1. A receipt will be sent electronically.
    2. The invoice number will be changed.
    3. A new payment method will be required.
    4. The account will be permanently closed.
  3. 3. Once the replacement parts _____, the technician will begin the repair.
    1. will arrive
    2. arrive
    3. arrived
    4. are arriving
  4. 4. Customers who fail to pay on time may _____ a processing fee.
    1. incur
    2. waive
    3. remit
    4. settle
顯示答案與解析
  1. B — 短文中的 avoid incurring additional charges 表示期限前付款是為了避免額外費用。
  2. A — Once payment is received 後，系統會寄出 electronic receipt。
  3. B — once 引導未來時間子句時使用現在簡單式 arrive；主要子句才使用 will begin。
  4. A — incur a fee 是固定搭配，表示「產生／招致費用」。
今日答題技巧：看到 once、when、as soon as 等時間連接詞時，先確認是否描述未來；時間子句仍使用現在式。閱讀題中的 outstanding 常改寫為 unpaid。
