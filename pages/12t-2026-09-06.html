2026年9月6日｜Transportation & Event Logistics

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • congestion (n.) 壅塞；擁擠
    heavy traffic congestion｜嚴重交通壅塞
  • reroute (v.) 改道；變更路線
    reroute the shuttle buses｜變更接駁車路線
  • dispatch (v.) 派遣；發送
    dispatch an additional vehicle｜加派一輛車
  • unforeseen (adj.) 未預見的
    unforeseen circumstances｜不可預見的情況
  • alternative (adj./n.) 替代的；替代方案
    an alternative route｜替代路線

2. 文法重點：so ... that 與 such ... that

so + 形容詞／副詞 + that 子句；such + (a/an) + 形容詞 + 名詞 + that 子句。兩者都表示「如此……以致於……」。

Traffic was so heavy that the bus arrived late.
It was such a busy morning that extra staff were needed.

3. 短文

Due to unforeseen road construction near the convention center, traffic congestion is expected throughout Monday morning. The usual shuttle route is so heavily affected that buses may arrive up to twenty minutes late. To reduce delays, the transportation coordinator has arranged such an efficient alternative route that most services should remain on schedule. An additional vehicle will also be dispatched between Central Station and the venue from 8:00 A.M. to 10:00 A.M. Passengers should allow extra travel time and check the event website before leaving.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why might the shuttle buses arrive late?
    1. A station will be temporarily closed.
    2. Road construction may cause congestion.
    3. The convention has been postponed.
    4. Drivers are attending a training session.
  2. 2. What will operate between Central Station and the venue?
    1. An additional vehicle
    2. A new railway service
    3. A temporary information desk
    4. A luggage delivery service
  3. 3. The demand was _____ high that the company extended its business hours.
    1. such
    2. so
    3. very much
    4. too much
  4. 4. The coordinator will _____ another bus if passenger numbers increase.
    1. dispatch
    2. congestion
    3. alternative
    4. unforeseen
顯示答案與解析
  1. B — 首句指出 unforeseen road construction 將造成交通壅塞，因此接駁車可能延誤。
  2. A — 文中明確指出 an additional vehicle 將在車站與會場間行駛。
  3. B — high 是形容詞，應使用 so + 形容詞 + that。
  4. A — will 後面需要原形動詞；dispatch another bus 表示「加派另一輛公車」。
今日答題技巧：空格後若直接接形容詞或副詞，優先考慮 so；若接「形容詞＋名詞」，優先考慮 such。閱讀題中的 allow extra travel time 表示應預留更多時間。
