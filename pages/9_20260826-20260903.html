DAY 1

2026-08-26｜despite 與 although

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • implement｜實施、執行
    implement a new policy
  • allocate｜分配
    allocate resources to a project
  • promptly｜迅速地、立即
    respond promptly
  • be eligible for｜符合……資格
    be eligible for a refund
  • in accordance with｜依照、根據
    in accordance with company policy

2. 文法重點

despite + 名詞／V-ing；although + 主詞 + 動詞。例：Despite the delay, the project was completed on time. / Although the shipment was delayed, it arrived before Friday.

3. 短文

Due to scheduled maintenance, the customer service system will be unavailable from 8:00 P.M. to 10:00 P.M. on Friday. Although employees will not be able to access customer records during this period, they may continue responding to general inquiries by e-mail. All pending requests should be processed promptly once the system is restored.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why will the system be unavailable?
    1. It is being replaced.
    2. Maintenance is scheduled.
    3. Customer records were lost.
    4. Employees are attending training.
  2. 2. What can employees do while the system is unavailable?
    1. Access customer records
    2. Process refund requests
    3. Answer general questions by e-mail
    4. Repair the system
  3. 3. All applications must be submitted _____ the stated procedures.
    1. according
    2. accordance
    3. in accordance with
    4. complying
  4. 4. _____ receiving several complaints, the company did not change its policy.
    1. Although
    2. Despite
    3. Because
    4. Therefore
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — scheduled maintenance 表示系統因例行維護而停用。
  2. 2. C — 員工仍可透過電子郵件回覆一般詢問。
  3. 3. C — 固定搭配為 in accordance with。
  4. 4. B — 後面是動名詞 receiving，因此使用 despite。
今日答題技巧：看到連接詞或介系詞後，先判斷後面是名詞／V-ing，還是完整的「主詞＋動詞」。
DAY 2

2026-08-27｜provided that 與 unless

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • reimburse｜報銷、償還
    reimburse employees for travel expenses
  • discrepancy｜差異、不一致
    a discrepancy in the invoice
  • tentative｜暫定的
    a tentative delivery date
  • on behalf of｜代表
    sign the contract on behalf of the company
  • subject to｜取決於；可能依……變更
    Prices are subject to change.

2. 文法重點

provided that + 子句表示「只要；條件是」；unless + 子句表示「除非」，相當於 if ... not。unless 本身已有否定含義，通常不再搭配 not。

3. 短文

The tentative delivery date for the new office equipment is September 5. However, the schedule is subject to change because several items are currently unavailable. The supplier will confirm the final date by Friday. Customers may cancel their orders without charge, provided that cancellation requests are submitted before shipment.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why might the delivery schedule change?
    1. The payment has not been received.
    2. Some products are unavailable.
    3. The supplier changed its office.
    4. Customers requested different equipment.
  2. 2. When will the supplier confirm the delivery date?
    1. Before shipment
    2. On September 5
    3. By Friday
    4. After receiving payment
  3. 3. Employees will not be _____ for taxi fares unless they provide valid receipts.
    1. reimbursement
    2. reimbursing
    3. reimbursed
    4. reimbursable
  4. 4. The discount will apply _____ the order is placed before August 31.
    1. unless
    2. provided that
    3. despite
    4. otherwise
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — several items are currently unavailable 是日期可能變動的原因。
  2. 2. C — by Friday 表示最晚在星期五前。
  3. 3. C — will not be reimbursed 是被動語態。
  4. 4. B — 折扣成立的條件是期限前下單。
今日答題技巧：詞性題先看句型骨架；will be 後若主詞是動作承受者，通常接過去分詞。
DAY 3

2026-08-28｜by 與 until

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • mandatory｜強制的、必須參加的
    mandatory safety training
  • facilitate｜促進；使更順利
    facilitate communication between departments
  • retain｜留住；保留
    retain experienced employees
  • in advance｜提前
    register at least two days in advance
  • no later than｜不遲於
    submit the form no later than Monday

2. 文法重點

by + 時間表示最晚在某時間前完成，強調截止點；until + 時間表示持續到某時間，強調持續期間。

3. 短文

All newly hired employees must attend a mandatory orientation session on September 12. The program is designed to facilitate communication between departments and familiarize employees with company procedures. Participants should confirm their attendance no later than September 8. Those requiring special assistance must notify the Human Resources Department in advance.

4. 題目

  1. 1. What is the main purpose of the orientation?
    1. To evaluate employee performance
    2. To introduce procedures and improve communication
    3. To recruit additional staff
    4. To revise company policies
  2. 2. By when should employees confirm their attendance?
    1. September 8
    2. September 12
    3. After the orientation
    4. On their first working day
  3. 3. The customer service center will remain closed _____ 9:00 A.M. on Monday.
    1. by
    2. until
    3. during
    4. within
  4. 4. The new scheduling system is expected to _____ cooperation among project teams.
    1. facilitation
    2. facilitator
    3. facilitate
    4. facilitated
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — 目的為介紹程序並促進部門溝通。
  2. 2. A — no later than September 8 等於 by September 8。
  3. 3. B — 關閉狀態會持續到上午九點。
  4. 4. C — to 後面需要原形動詞。
今日答題技巧：remain、stay、be open/closed 常搭配 until；提交、完成或付款的期限通常使用 by。

2026-08-29｜have + 受詞 + 過去分詞

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • renovation｜翻修、整修
    office renovation work
  • disruption｜中斷；干擾
    minimize disruption to operations
  • adjacent｜鄰近的
    the adjacent conference room
  • temporarily｜暫時地
    temporarily relocate the staff
  • make arrangements for｜安排、籌備
    make arrangements for alternative workspace

2. 文法重點

have + 受詞 + 過去分詞表示「請／安排別人完成某事」。例：The company had the office renovated. / We will have the equipment inspected tomorrow.

3. 短文

Renovation of the third-floor offices will begin next Monday and is expected to last two weeks. To minimize disruption, employees assigned to that floor will temporarily work in the adjacent training center. The Facilities Department will have all computers moved on Friday evening. Employees should take personal belongings home before 5:00 P.M. on Friday.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why will some employees work in the training center?
    1. Their computers are being upgraded.
    2. Their offices are being renovated.
    3. A training program is being held.
    4. The third floor will permanently close.
  2. 2. What should employees do before Friday evening?
    1. Move their own computers
    2. Complete the renovation
    3. Remove their personal belongings
    4. Contact the training department
  3. 3. The manager will have the final contract _____ by a legal adviser.
    1. review
    2. reviewed
    3. reviewing
    4. reviews
  4. 4. The temporary closure caused little _____ to normal business operations.
    1. disrupt
    2. disruptive
    3. disruption
    4. disruptively
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — 第三樓辦公室正在整修。
  2. 2. C — 員工應在星期五下午五點前帶走私人用品。
  3. 3. B — have + 受詞 + 過去分詞。
  4. 4. C — little 後面需要名詞。
今日答題技巧：看到 have/get + 受詞 + 空格，若受詞是被處理的對象，空格通常選過去分詞。

2026-08-30｜現在分詞與過去分詞修飾名詞

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • comply with｜遵守
    comply with security regulations
  • confidential｜機密的
    confidential customer data
  • authorization｜授權、許可
    obtain authorization from a supervisor
  • undergo｜接受、經歷
    undergo a security audit
  • be responsible for｜負責
    be responsible for protecting information

2. 文法重點

V-ing 表示名詞主動執行動作；過去分詞表示名詞被執行動作。例：Employees handling customer data / Files stored on the server。

3. 短文

Employees handling confidential customer information must complete an online security course by September 15. The course covers procedures designed to prevent unauthorized access and data loss. Staff members who have already undergone equivalent training may request an exemption. However, the request must be approved by their department manager.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Who must complete the security course?
    1. All customers
    2. Employees who handle confidential information
    3. Only department managers
    4. Staff responsible for course design
  2. 2. What may previously trained employees request?
    1. Additional authorization
    2. A course extension
    3. An exemption
    4. A department transfer
  3. 3. Customers _____ to update their account details should complete this form.
    1. wish
    2. wished
    3. wishing
    4. wishes
  4. 4. All documents _____ by external consultants must be reviewed by management.
    1. prepare
    2. preparing
    3. prepared
    4. preparation
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — 首句直接指出處理機密資訊的員工必須完成課程。
  2. 2. C — 接受過同等訓練者可申請 exemption。
  3. 3. C — 顧客主動希望更新資料，因此用 wishing。
  4. 4. C — 文件被顧問準備，因此用 prepared。
今日答題技巧：名詞主動執行動作用 V-ing；名詞承受動作用過去分詞。

2026-08-31｜not only ... but also ...

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • prospective｜潛在的、未來的
    prospective clients
  • demonstrate｜展現、證明
    demonstrate technical expertise
  • proficiency｜熟練、精通
    proficiency in data analysis
  • meet the requirements｜符合要求
    meet the job requirements
  • in addition to｜除了……之外還
    in addition to technical skills

2. 文法重點

not only ... but also ... 表示「不僅……而且……」，兩側必須使用平行結構。例：The position requires not only technical knowledge but also communication skills.

3. 短文

BrightPath Consulting is seeking a junior business analyst to join its Taipei office. The successful candidate will not only analyze operational data but also document business processes. Applicants should demonstrate proficiency in spreadsheet software and possess strong problem-solving skills. Experience with SQL is preferred but not required.

4. 題目

  1. 1. What is one responsibility of the successful candidate?
    1. Training senior consultants
    2. Documenting business processes
    3. Developing spreadsheet software
    4. Managing the Taipei office
  2. 2. What is indicated about SQL experience?
    1. It is mandatory.
    2. It will be taught before the interview.
    3. It is desirable but not essential.
    4. It replaces the need for a résumé.
  3. 3. The new system will not only reduce processing time _____ improve data accuracy.
    1. but also
    2. as well
    3. and yet
    4. in addition
  4. 4. Candidates must demonstrate technical _____ during the assessment.
    1. proficient
    2. proficiently
    3. proficiency
    4. proficiencies
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — 短文指出需記錄業務流程。
  2. 2. C — preferred but not required 表示最好具備，但並非必要。
  3. 3. A — 固定搭配為 not only ... but also ...。
  4. 4. C — demonstrate 後面需要名詞 proficiency。
今日答題技巧：看到 not only 時先找 but also，再檢查兩側是否為相同詞性或句型。

2026-09-01｜建議／要求後的 that 子句

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • contingency｜突發狀況；應變事件
    a contingency plan
  • feasible｜可行的
    a feasible solution
  • consensus｜共識
    reach a consensus
  • postpone｜延後、延期
    postpone the meeting
  • notify｜通知
    notify participants of a change

2. 文法重點

recommend / suggest / request / require + that + 主詞 + 原形動詞。被動語態使用 be + 過去分詞。例：The manager recommended that the meeting be postponed.

3. 短文

Due to an unexpected scheduling conflict, the product demonstration planned for Thursday may need to be postponed. The project manager has recommended that the event be moved to the following Tuesday. Before making a final decision, however, she would like the sales and technical teams to confirm whether the new date is feasible. Team leaders should notify her of any concerns by noon tomorrow. Once a consensus has been reached, all registered clients will receive an updated invitation.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why might the product demonstration be postponed?
    1. The venue is being renovated.
    2. A scheduling conflict has occurred.
    3. Clients requested a different product.
    4. The technical team is unavailable next Tuesday.
  2. 2. What must happen before clients receive an updated invitation?
    1. A consensus must be reached.
    2. The product must be redesigned.
    3. The venue must be inspected.
    4. A new project manager must be appointed.
  3. 3. The director requested that the revised proposal _____ before the meeting.
    1. submits
    2. submitted
    3. be submitted
    4. is submitting
  4. 4. The committee must determine whether the schedule is financially _____.
    1. feasibility
    2. feasible
    3. feasibly
    4. feasibleness
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — 原因是 unexpected scheduling conflict。
  2. 2. A — 達成共識後才會寄出更新邀請。
  3. 3. C — request that 後用原形；proposal 被提交，所以用 be submitted。
  4. 4. B — is 後面需要形容詞作主詞補語。
今日答題技巧：看到 recommend、suggest、request、require 時，優先檢查 that 子句是否使用原形動詞。

2026-09-02｜each、every 與 one of

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • anticipated｜預期的
    higher-than-anticipated demand
  • substantial｜大量的；重大的
    a substantial increase
  • inventory｜庫存
    check the available inventory
  • accommodate｜滿足、容納
    accommodate additional orders
  • remainder｜剩餘部分
    the remainder of the shipment

2. 文法重點

each / every + 單數名詞 + 單數動詞；one of + 複數名詞 + 單數動詞。動詞要與 each、every 或 one 對應，不要被 of 後面的複數名詞干擾。

3. 短文

Demand for the newly released EcoBlend coffee maker has been substantially higher than anticipated. Although each of our retail locations receives a weekly shipment, current inventory remains limited. To accommodate additional orders, the manufacturer has agreed to increase production beginning next week. Customers whose orders cannot be filled immediately will receive an estimated delivery date by e-mail. The remainder of the orders is expected to be shipped within ten business days.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why is the product currently in limited supply?
    1. Several stores stopped selling it.
    2. Demand exceeded expectations.
    3. Weekly shipments were canceled.
    4. The manufacturer reduced production.
  2. 2. What will customers with delayed orders receive?
    1. A discount coupon
    2. A replacement product
    3. An estimated delivery date
    4. A store inventory report
  3. 3. Each of the regional managers _____ responsible for reviewing the sales figures.
    1. are
    2. were
    3. is
    4. have been
  4. 4. One of the warehouse employees _____ the inventory report every Friday.
    1. prepare
    2. prepares
    3. preparing
    4. have prepared
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — higher than anticipated 表示需求超出預期。
  2. 2. C — 延遲訂單的顧客會收到 estimated delivery date。
  3. 3. C — each 是核心主詞，因此使用 is。
  4. 4. B — 核心主詞是 one，因此使用 prepares。
今日答題技巧：看到 each of 或 one of，暫時忽略 of 後面的複數名詞，先找真正決定動詞單複數的核心主詞。

2026-09-03｜either ... or／neither ... nor

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • renewal｜續約；更新
    contract renewal
  • terminate｜終止
    terminate an agreement
  • binding｜有約束力的
    a legally binding contract
  • negotiate｜協商、談判
    negotiate favorable terms
  • expire｜到期、失效
    The subscription expires next month.

2. 文法重點

either ... or 與 neither ... nor 連接兩個主詞時，動詞通常與最靠近它的主詞一致。例：Either the manager or the assistants are attending. / Either the assistants or the manager is attending.

3. 短文

Northgate Solutions’ maintenance contract with Apex Systems will expire on September 30. Although the service has generally been reliable, recent fee increases have prompted management to review other providers. Either the Procurement Department or the IT director will negotiate revised terms with Apex next week. If neither a lower rate nor additional technical support is offered, Northgate may terminate the agreement and select another provider. A final decision will be announced before the renewal deadline.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Why is Northgate reviewing other service providers?
    1. The system has frequently failed.
    2. Apex recently raised its fees.
    3. The contract has already been terminated.
    4. The IT director is leaving the company.
  2. 2. What may Northgate do if Apex offers neither a lower rate nor extra support?
    1. Extend the current contract automatically.
    2. Delay all maintenance work.
    3. Choose a different provider.
    4. Hire additional IT employees.
  3. 3. Either the department heads or the chief operating officer _____ required to approve the renewal.
    1. are
    2. were
    3. is
    4. have
  4. 4. Neither the current proposal nor the alternative plans _____ financially acceptable.
    1. is
    2. are
    3. was
    4. has been
顯示答案與解析
  1. 1. B — recent fee increases 促使管理層評估其他供應商。
  2. 2. C — 公司可能終止原合約並改選供應商。
  3. 3. C — 最近的主詞 chief operating officer 是單數。
  4. 4. B — 最近的主詞 alternative plans 是複數。
今日答題技巧：先圈出離動詞最近的主詞，再決定單複數；review other providers 是 consider alternatives 的同義改寫。
