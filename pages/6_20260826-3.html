📚 實用字彙

implement (v.) 實施、執行
implement a new policy — 實施新政策
allocate (v.) 分配
allocate resources to a project — 為專案分配資源
promptly (adv.) 迅速地、立即
respond promptly — 迅速回覆
be eligible for 符合……資格
be eligible for a refund — 符合退款資格
in accordance with 依照、根據
in accordance with company policy — 依照公司政策

📚 詞彙延伸：Self-fulfilling Prophecy

self-fulfilling prophecy 自我應驗預言
Her belief that she would fail the interview led her to be so nervous that she indeed performed poorly.
她相信面試會失敗，這讓她極度緊張，結果她真的表現得很差。
Pygmalion effect 皮格馬利翁效應
The teacher's high expectations for her students often led to their improved academic performance.
老師對學生的高度期望，往往會促使他們提升學業表現。
expectation (n.) 預期
His low expectations for the project made him less motivated to work hard on it.
他對這個專案的低預期讓他缺乏努力工作的動力。

✏️ 文法重點：despite 與 although

despite + 名詞／V-ing　　although + 主詞 + 動詞
Despite the delay, the project was completed on time.
Although the shipment was delayed, it arrived before Friday.
❌ Despite the shipment was delayed
✅ Despite the shipment being delayed

📄 短文

點卡片翻面看中文，或點喇叭朗讀
Due to scheduled maintenance, the customer service system will be unavailable from 8:00 P.M. to 10:00 P.M. on Friday. Although employees will not be able to access customer records during this period, they may continue responding to general inquiries by e-mail. All pending requests should be processed promptly once the system is restored.
👆 點卡片看中文翻譯
由於系統排定進行維護，客服系統將於週五晚上 8:00 至 10:00 停止服務。雖然員工在此期間將無法讀取客戶紀錄，但仍可繼續透過電子郵件回覆一般性詢問。系統恢復運作後，所有待處理的請求應立即處理。

❓ 題目（點選作答，答對答錯都會顯示解析）

1. Why will the system be unavailable?
B — scheduled maintenance 表示系統因例行維護而停用。
2. What can employees do while the system is unavailable?
C — 文中指出員工仍可透過電子郵件回覆一般詢問。
3. All applications must be submitted _____ the stated procedures.
C — 固定搭配為 in accordance with，意思是「依照」。
4. _____ receiving several complaints, the company did not change its policy.
B — 後面是動名詞 receiving，因此使用 despite；although 後面必須接完整子句。
💡 答題技巧：看到介系詞後，先確認後面應接名詞或 V-ing；若後面有完整的「主詞＋動詞」，才考慮 although。
