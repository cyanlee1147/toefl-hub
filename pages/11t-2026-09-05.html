2026年9月5日｜Customer Survey & Feedback

1. 實用字彙與片語

  • respondent (n.) 問卷受訪者
    survey respondents｜問卷受訪者
  • confidentiality (n.) 保密性
    ensure confidentiality｜確保資料保密
  • incentive (n.) 獎勵；誘因
    offer an incentive｜提供獎勵
  • compile (v.) 彙整；編製
    compile survey results｜彙整問卷結果
  • insight (n.) 洞察；深入理解
    gain valuable insights｜獲得寶貴洞察

2. 文法重點：關係代名詞

who 用於人；which 用於事物；that 可用於人或事物；whose 表示「某人的／某物的」，後面接名詞。

Customers who complete the survey will receive a coupon.
Respondents whose answers are selected will be contacted.

3. 短文

Customers who purchased a HomePro appliance during the past six months are invited to complete a brief online survey. The questionnaire, which takes approximately five minutes to finish, asks about product quality and delivery service. Respondents whose answers are submitted by September 12 will receive a discount code for their next purchase. All responses will remain anonymous to ensure confidentiality. The research team will compile the results and present its findings to managers who are responsible for improving the customer experience.

4. 題目

  1. 1. Who is invited to complete the survey?
    1. All HomePro employees
    2. Customers who recently purchased an appliance
    3. Delivery drivers who work for HomePro
    4. Managers responsible for product design
  2. 2. What will qualified respondents receive?
    1. A replacement appliance
    2. A cash payment
    3. A discount code
    4. A printed survey report
  3. 3. Employees _____ performance exceeds expectations may receive a bonus.
    1. who
    2. whose
    3. which
    4. whom
  4. 4. The analyst will _____ the responses before preparing the presentation.
    1. compilation
    2. compile
    3. compiled
    4. compiler
顯示答案與解析
  1. B — 首句指出，受邀者是過去六個月內購買 HomePro 家電的顧客。
  2. C — 在期限前提交答案的受訪者會收到下一次購物使用的 discount code。
  3. B — 空格表示 employees 的 performance，因此需要表所有關係的 whose。
  4. B — will 後面需要原形動詞 compile，表示「彙整回覆」。
今日答題技巧：先看先行詞是人或事物，再判斷空格後是否已有名詞；空格後若緊接名詞，通常優先考慮 whose。閱讀題中的 invited to complete the survey 可改寫為 asked to participate。
