📚 實用字彙與片語

reimburse (v.) 報銷、償還
reimburse employees for travel expenses — 為員工報銷差旅費用
discrepancy (n.) 差異、不一致
a discrepancy in the invoice — 發票上的差異
tentative (adj.) 暫定的
a tentative delivery date — 暫定的交貨日期
on behalf of 代表
sign the contract on behalf of the company — 代表公司簽署合約
subject to 取決於；可能依……變更
Prices are subject to change. — 價格可能會變動。

📚 詞彙延伸：Self-fulfilling Prophecy

self-fulfilling prophecy 自我應驗預言
Her belief that she would fail the interview led her to be so nervous that she indeed performed poorly.
她相信面試會失敗，這讓她極度緊張，結果她真的表現得很差。
Pygmalion effect 皮格馬利翁效應
The teacher's high expectations for her students often led to their improved academic performance.
老師對學生的高度期望，往往會促使他們提升學業表現。
expectation (n.) 預期
His low expectations for the project made him less motivated to work hard on it.
他對這個專案的低預期讓他缺乏努力工作的動力。

✏️ 文法重點：provided that 與 unless

provided that + 子句：只要；條件是
unless + 子句：除非，相當於 if ... not
Expenses will be reimbursed provided that receipts are submitted.
Your reservation will be canceled unless payment is received today.
注意：unless 本身已有否定含義，通常不再搭配 not。

The tentative delivery date for the new office equipment is September 5. However, the schedule is subject to change because several items are currently unavailable. The supplier will confirm the final date by Friday. Customers may cancel their orders without charge, provided that cancellation requests are submitted before shipment.

❓ 題目（點選作答，答對答錯都會顯示解析）

1. Why might the delivery schedule change?
B — several items are currently unavailable 是日期可能變動的原因。
2. When will the supplier confirm the delivery date?
C — by Friday 表示「最晚在星期五前」，是 TOEIC 常見時間表達。
3. Employees will not be _____ for taxi fares unless they provide valid receipts.
C — will not be reimbursed 是被動語態，表示員工「不會獲得報銷」。
4. The discount will apply _____ the order is placed before August 31.
B — 折扣成立的條件是 8 月 31 日前下單，因此用 provided that。
💡 答題技巧：遇到詞性題時，先看句型骨架。第 3 題已有 will not be，後面需要過去分詞組成被動語態，不必先翻譯每個選項。
